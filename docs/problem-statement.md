# Problem Statement

## Problem

Develop a free, two-minute oral health screening widget for web or smartphones that guides patients through a simple set of prompts and captures five quick images of their teeth.

The solution should analyse these images and generate an instant visual report highlighting potential oral health concerns such as crooked teeth, tooth wear, or discoloration. The goal is to provide patients with an easy, accessible way to get an initial visual assessment of their oral health and understand whether they may need to consult a dentist.

## Why It Matters

People often notice visible dental changes before they feel pain. Common examples include stains, chips, crowding, gum redness, plaque buildup, and worn edges.

Most people do not know whether these visible changes are urgent, mild, or normal. toothpaste.cv gives them a simple first step before a dental visit: guided photos, a plain report, and a clear next action.

## Project Goal

The goal is not to replace a dentist. The goal is to make early awareness easier.

toothpaste.cv helps users:

- Capture five useful oral photos in a guided flow.
- Get a quick visual screening report.
- Understand which visible issues may need attention.
- Save or export a dentist-ready summary.
- Track changes over time when they choose to save reports.

## Screening Categories

- Crowding and alignment
- Tooth wear
- Discoloration and staining
- Gum health
- Plaque and tartar buildup
- Bite alignment
- Spacing and gaps
- Chips and fractures
- Possible decay

## Product Boundary

toothpaste.cv is a visual screening tool only. It is not a medical diagnosis, treatment plan, X-ray replacement, or certified medical device.

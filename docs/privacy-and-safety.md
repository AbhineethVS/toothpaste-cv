# Privacy And Safety

## Safety Boundary

toothpaste.cv provides preliminary visual screening only.

It is not:

- A medical diagnosis
- A treatment plan
- A substitute for a licensed dentist
- An X-ray or clinical exam replacement
- A certified medical device

Users should consult a dentist for pain, swelling, bleeding, trauma, suspected cavities, or any concern that does not improve.

## What The App Can Screen

The app can comment on visible surface-level signs in photos:

- Crowding and alignment
- Tooth wear
- Discoloration and staining
- Gum redness or swelling
- Plaque or tartar buildup
- Bite alignment
- Spacing and gaps
- Chips and fractures
- Possible decay spots

## What The App Cannot See

The app cannot reliably assess:

- Cavities between teeth
- Decay below the surface
- Gum pocket depth
- Root or nerve issues
- Bone loss
- Infection status
- Treatment needs

These require a dentist and clinical tools.

## Photo Handling

- A screening does not require an account.
- Live photos are used to generate the report.
- Photos are sent to configured AI providers for analysis.
- The app does not permanently store photos for the free screening flow.
- Saved reports are only kept when the user chooses to save them.

## AI Output Handling

- The report uses a fixed oral-health rubric.
- Output is validated against a structured schema.
- Findings use severity labels: none, mild, moderate, and notable.
- When uncertain, the product should stay conservative and recommend dentist review rather than overstate a diagnosis.

## User Consent

The flow shows Terms and Privacy before capture. The product should continue to make the screening-only boundary visible in the app and report.
